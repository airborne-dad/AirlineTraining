# Techniques

- **Get configured early on LOC approaches.**  
  It gets busy very quickly, so being ahead of the aircraft is crucial.

- **DDA (Derived Decision Altitude)**  
  If the approach has a VNAV path (dotted line down from the MAP), you can use the APP button to arm LOC and a green GP.

- **Handling SIDs with Expected Vectors**  
  - If the SID contains descriptions for expected vectors and routing, it might not appear in the database unless it's an RNAV SID.  
  - In such cases, you can manually enter them into the FPL (e.g. LaGuardia X Departure lists transitions and expected vectors to VOR radials not loaded in the FMS).

- **Approach Briefing**  
  - **STAR**: Display the FPL page on the MCDU. First, verify the runway with the chart, then check each fix if it has altitude and speed restrictions, comparing them with the chart.  
  - **APP**: Same procedure — verify all fixes in the FPL align with the approach chart.

- **Holding**  
  - Upon entering a hold, PM should request “speed our discretion” from ATC.  
  - Leaving the hold, PM requests resuming normal speed.

- **Go-Around on a LOC Approach**  
  - Make sure to *click the FMS buttons on the PF side* during the go-around because the Source selector is on the PF side (which is on V/L instead of FMS). When PF calls for FMS NAV, the autopilot won’t know what to do if you don’t switch it correctly.

- **CAMI Communication**  
  - Use clear language. Instead of saying “Does this look good?” say **“Please confirm!”** to prompt a formal check.

- **Briefing Timing**  
  1. **Crew** – after power on.  
  2. **Release** – prior to departure review.  
  3. **Departure** – after To the Line Flow is completed and release briefing is done.  
  4. **Approach** – before Descent Checklist.  
  5. **Landing** – after brakes set.

- **Departure Route and Altitude**  
  - After building out the departure route, remember to bug the top altitude.

- **Comm Setup**  
  - Ensure radios are correctly tuned for the departure, approach, or any special procedures.

- **QRC Actions**  
  - CA will call for QRC actions, FO performs them. The CA is usually busy coordinating with ground, dealing with a delay, or passenger updates.

- **Addressing AFML Discrepancies In-Flight**  
  - If there’s time to write down discrepancies mid-flight, do so.  
  - If not, wait until after landing and parked, then call Dispatch to connect with Maintenance.

- **Descent Checklist**  
  - CA will notify FAs regarding the descent.  
  - Then they call for the Descent Checklist, so complete your flow before that.
