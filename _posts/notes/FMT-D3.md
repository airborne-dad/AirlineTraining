# FMP 3 Notes

## 🌬️ Crosswind Takeoff
- Apply **forward control wheel pressure** to keep the nosewheel on the runway until rotation. Be assertive.
- Once airborne, **release rudder pressure** and let the aircraft naturally weather-vane into the wind. Follow TOGA ref lines.

## 🛫 RNAV SID
- **TRIM proactively!** Single click sometimes isn't enough. Use 2-second bursts if necessary.
- Stay ahead on **callouts**—intentional, step-by-step, good cadence.
- Clean up the aircraft promptly to avoid overspeed risk.

## 🧭 LDA (LOC) Approach
- Treat like any other LOC—just be methodical:
  - Early configuration is okay.
  - Passing the fix prior to FAF:
    1. Set **MDA**
    2. Select **V/L**
    3. Select **NAV**
- Let the aircraft follow the **3° descent path**—monitor and fine-tune as needed.

## 👁️ Visual Approach
- Divide focus between **outside visuals** and **instruments**.
- Use the **Flight Path Vector (FPV)**—top barb touching the horizon ≈ 3° glide.
- Maintain strong **centerline discipline**.

## 💥 V1 Cuts
- Treat it almost like a visual departure:
  1. Eyes on runway—be patient.
  2. Count **3 seconds** before rotation.
  3. Apply **rudder pressure** and gently rotate.
  4. Keep visual reference on the runway during rotation.
  5. Begin **SCAN**: Black square → Heading bug → TOGA bars → Wings level.
  6. Heading bug is **critical**—stay on it.
  7. Maintain **10° nose-up pitch**, wait for **V2 reschedule line** to catch up.
  8. After FLCH VFS call → **Autopilot ON**.
  9. Maintain rudder pressure throughout.

## 🔁 Single Engine Go-Around
- Smoothly apply **TOGA thrust**—be assertive.
- Keep a **linear relationship** between **rudder input** and **thrust lever**:
  - Thrust lever forward → Rudder forward
  - Thrust lever back → Ease rudder

## 🛬 Single Engine Approach & Landing
- Call **appropriate checklists**.
- Maintain consistent **rudder pressure** throughout.
- Bring power to **idle at 50 ft AGL** and complete a normal landing.
