# FMT 4 Notes

## 🛫 Flaps 1 Takeoff
- During the ground roll, apply **forward pressure** on the control wheel to help keep the nose down.
- At rotation, **rotate assertively** to the **V2 line**.
- **Trim promptly** after liftoff.
- If the **airspeed indication is suspected to be unreliable**, command and fly **appropriate manual speeds**.

## 💥 V2 Cut
- Quickly recognize the **yawing tendency** and correct with rudder input.
- Immediately center on the **heading bug**.
- Lower the nose to realign with the **new V2 line**.
- Stabilize aircraft first, **then call: `HDG, BANK`**.

## 👁️ Visual Approach
- Use **HDG mode** to assist in intercepting the **final approach course** if vectors are not provided.
- Alternatively:
  - Use **FMS navigation** if applicable.
  - Or fly **visual** if operating in **VMC**.
