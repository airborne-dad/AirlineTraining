# FMT 1 - Training Notes

## 1. Normal Takeoff
- On rotation, avoid aggressively pitching up. Find a **~3° per second** cadence to align with **TOGA reference lines**.
- After **VNAV activates**, TOGA lines disappear — follow **Flight Director**.
- Leave power at **TOGA** until **ASEL** appears on the FMA.
- Then **reduce power gradually** by referencing the **speed trend vector** (white bar right of the airspeed tape).

## 2. Climbing Turn
- Choose vertical mode based on performance needs:
  - **FLCH**: Use TOGA power, level off at ASEL, and reduce thrust referencing speed trend.
  - **VS/FPA**: Adjust thrust as needed — these are **SPDt** modes.
- Understand how FD behavior changes by mode. Do not over-use the throttle. Set what you want and see what happens.

## 3. Descending Turn
- Expect **power near idle**, regardless of vertical mode.

## 4. Steep Turns
- Enter a **45° bank**.
- Add ~**3% thrust** (small, precise adjustment).(My technique: adjust one throttle slightly, then match with the other. Hard to fine-tune both at the same time. No leverage.)
- Maintain by **slicing the horizon with the Flight Path Vector (green circle)**.

## 5. Clean Stall Recovery
1. **Callouts**: Either pilot: "Stall", "Out of stall", PF: “Recover”, PM: “HDG & FLCH 210”.
2. **Silence AP warning**.
3. **Pitch nose down** slightly below the horizon. Then start pitching up, respecting PLI. Stop at +10 degrees and let the plane catch up.
4. If significant altitude loss: **Full power**, then level off smoothly.

## 6. Landing Stall Recovery
- On "Recover", PF calls for **Flaps 2 or 4**.
- PM: “Positive Rate” → PF: **"Gear Up"**. Then PM will set HDG and FLCH 210.
- Proceed with level-off and recovery.

## 7. RA (Resolution Advisory)
- Follow the **green guidance boxes**.
- Press and **hold TCS button** until resolved.
- PM to ATC:
  - “Brickyard XXXX responding to an RA.”
  - After: “Brickyard XXXX, conflict resolved.”

## 8. ILS CAT I
- Turn on the center taxi light when cleared for the approach and the center landing light when cleared to land.
- Configure up to **Flaps 2** before GS capture.
- Once GS captured: **Flaps 3 and Gear Down**.
- PM presses the **ATTND button** for FA notification.

## 9. ILS CAT I with AP FAIL
- **Trim and hand-fly** using FD.
- Eyes inside until PM: “Runway in sight”, then eyes out and track **PAPI**.
- Expect minimal flare — descent profile is already **3° nose-up**.
- RA cadence: **50-40-30-20-10**
  - **Idle power at 30 ft**, gentle back pressure.

## 10. Visual Approaches
- One word: **TRIM**.
- Maintain smooth control through coordinated trim and attitude management.
