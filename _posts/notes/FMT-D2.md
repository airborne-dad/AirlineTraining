# FMT 2 Notes

## 🛬 ILS CAT II
- Ensure both sides are set to **RA minimums**.
- Verify **LOC identifier** on the MCDU PROG page.
- Use **PREV**.
- Manually bug the course if not auto-tuned.
- Brief it like a conversation.
- Run appropriate checklists: descent, runway change, etc.

## ⛔ ILS CAT II Missed Approach
- Immediately select: `FMS → NAV` (**Critical step**)
- Then: `FLCH 210`
- If LNAV fails to capture, assist with **HDG mode**.

## ✅ ILS CAT II Landing
- Standard landing.
- Disconnect AP and hand fly the rest as normal.

## 🧭 RNAV SID
- Stay on top of the **Flight Director** at all times.

## 📡 RNAV Approach
- Normal procedure.
- Think **Reverse C**. Remember to nbug **FAF altitude** upon clearance:
  - `NAV → VNAV → ASEL → APP`

## 👀 Visual Approach
- Split attention: outside vs instruments.
- Cross-check continuously.
- Monitor and maintain **FPA**.
- Be pitch-aware and speed-conscious.
- Follow PAPI. Stay on glidepath. Don't dip below.

## 💥 V1 Cut
1. Keep visual ref, align or stay parallel to centerline.
2. Count to **3** before rotating. Buy time to stabilize.
3. Rotate **smoothly and intentionally**. Stop at 10 degrees nose pitch and wait for V2 line to reschedule.
4. Apply more **rudder** airborne due to loss of ground friction.
5. **SCAN**:
   - Black square (nose)
   - TOGA bars (V2)
   - Heading bug (within the edges of the bug)
   - Turn coordinator (brick)
6. Lock in rudder pressure, ONLY use small adjustments.
7. Use ailerons to level the wings.
8. Maintain standard **callouts**.
9. Once stable at safe altitude, **Autopilot ON**.

## 🛬 Single Engine Approach (Dry Method)
1. GS alive → **Flaps 1**
2. GS 2nd dot → **190 kts**, Flaps 2
3. GS 1st dot → **170 kts**
4. GS capture → **Flaps 3, Gear down**
5. Power: **~65% N1**
6. Flaps 5 → Vapp → Landing checklist

## 🛬 Single Engine Landing
- Expect **excess thrust** and speed.
- Power to idle at **100 ft**.
- Fly a **normal profile**.
- Only one **reverser**—plan rollout accordingly.

## 🚨 PRM Breakouts

### Climb Breakout (TOGA):
- PF: `Breakout, TOGA.`
- PM: Set HDG and FLCH 210
- PF: Level off on assigned heading → `Flaps 2`
- PM: `Positive rate.`
- PM: `Gear Up → Flaps 1 → Flaps Up → After TO checklist`

### Descend Breakout:
- Compliant once on assigned heading.
- Clean up:
  - Flaps 2
  - Gear Up
  - Flaps 1 → Flaps Up
