# ✈ Maneuvers Validation Notes

## General Overview
- **Focus:** Only the **maneuvers** are being tested.
- **Flight Plan & Setup:** Done **for you**—no need to touch MCDU or guidance panel unless you want to. Don't hesitate to delegate. (Checked with the examiner just to confirm and he said per the SOP, you don't have to do the guidance panel at all if you don't want to, just that if the AP isn't on, you definitely want to delegate.) I did the guidance panel and preview setup (ILS) just because I'm used to it. 
- We got KATL runway 9R for everything (9L for rejected t/os). But really no need to look at the plates. Don't really need them tbh. Don't let any expectation bias creep in.
- **Briefing**
  - Examiner detailed briefing slides—**pay close attention**. Very straightforward. No gotchas!
  - Same redo policy as Procedures Validation:
    - One maneuver **twice**, or two maneuvers **once** if you had to redo any.
  - **Minor deviations** are OK **if you're aware** and correcting—this will just be a **debrief item**.

---

## 🛫 Validation Flow (In Order)

### 1. Low Visibility Crosswind Takeoff (RVR 1600, 10 kt X-wind)
- Do a normal takeoff briefing to make sure everyone is on the same page.
- Perform takeoff normally.
-  RNAV SID.
- **Autopilot on** at 1000 ft.
- Trim and callouts matter!

---

### 2. ILS CAT II Approach
- You’ll be vectored for setup—**brief and review** the approach.
- The **SPC brief** is already done during the initial briefing in the briefing room, so it's considered done here.
- CA will program MCDU and begin TPC briefing with their threats, then your threats, then they will brief the approach.
- Remember to set **RA mins**.
- You will call descent checklist and the examiner will say that's completed.
- **Autopilot + Autothrottle** will stay on for this.
- **Missed Approach:** Use correct mode at 400 ft (`FMS NAV` or `HDG`) as instructed.
  - If unsure—**ask your Captain**.

---

### 3. RNAV Approach (Hand-flown from FAF)
- Switch back to **Baro mins** and stay there for the rest of the validation.
- Autopilot ON, Autothrottle ON **until FAF**.
- At **FAF**:
  - Autopilot OFF
  - Autothrottle stays ON
- Hand fly down to **50 ft**, then **Go-Around** on examiner's call.
  - **Listen closely** for go-around instructions (Runway HDG vs FMS NAV).
  - Clarify with CA if needed.

---

### 4. Flap Fail Scenario
- Vectored for another RNAV.
- Examiner instructed for spacing slowdown to **170 kts**.
- Call for flaps 1 before slowing down so green dot reschedules.
- **Flap Fail** EICAS triggers:
  - Identify and cancel.
  - Ask CA to request **210 kts**.
  - Call for **QRH Flap Fail**.
  - CA will quickly go through it and consider it done.
- Vectored back for RNAV:
  - Autopilot OFF at FAF.
  - Autothrottle ON.
- Full stop landing—use **No Flap Landing Technique**.

---

### 5. V1 Cut
- Repositioned to same runway.
- Perform V1 cut.
- Call **Autopilot ON** after FLCH Vfs callout.
- **Trim as needed**—remember to take trim **out** to a comfort level before landing.
- Tell your CA to ask for alternative missed approach instructions, runway heading, 4000ft or something.

---

### 6. Single-Engine ILS to Missed
- Vectored for ILS.
- Full callouts, fly the approach.
- **Missed Approach** at mins:
  - Fly **Runway HDG**. 
  - Continue standard missed approach procedure.

---

### 7. Single-Engine ILS to Landing
- Vectored for the same ILS approach again.
- At FAF:
  - Autopilot OFF
  - Autothrottle ON
- Perform **single-engine landing**.

---

### 8. Rejected Takeoff – PM Role
- CA is PF.
- You monitor ONLY and:
  - Make **callouts**.
  - Communicate with **ATC**:
    - `"Tower, Brickyard xxx, rejected takeoff on Runway xx."`
  - Make **PA announcement**:
    - `"Ladies and gentlemen, we have rejected our takeoff. Please remain seated with your seatbelts fastened. Follow the instructions of the uniformed flight attendants and standby for further instructions."`
- **Brake temp check is no longer required**.
- Request taxi off runway.
- Swap seats with CA.

---

### 9. Rejected Takeoff – PF Role
- You are now **CA and PF**.
- Perform takeoff.
- Examiner calls "REJECT."
- Execute full **rejected takeoff** procedure. Full stop on the runway.

---

### 10. Taxi Operations
- If you go first, you will taxi back to the runway for your partner.
- If you go second, you will taxi to the gate (B20).
- Examiner and CA will **guide you**—no need to review taxi charts.
- This is just an **FAA checkbox** item—**don’t stress**.

---

## ✅ Tips for Success
- Let the CA help—**you’re not expected to do it all**.
- Confirm instructions if anything is unclear.
- Focus on staying ahead of the aircraft and correcting trends.
- Most importantly—**fly safely, fly smoothly, and communicate**!
